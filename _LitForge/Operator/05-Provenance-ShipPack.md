# Provenance, Logs, and the Ship Pack

## Why logs exist

LitForge logs exist to answer:
- What changed?
- Who changed it (human vs model vs tool)?
- When and why?
- What checks passed?

This matters for:
- personal quality control
- collaboration/editors
- compliance narratives (where applicable)

## Reality check

Logs are best-effort. Users can delete them.
LitForge’s job is to make the truth **easy to keep** and **hard to accidentally lose**.

## Ship Pack (planned)

A Ship Pack is a structured export bundle that can include:
- final manuscript/script
- synopsis + positioning
- outline + key decisions
- claims table (NF)
- changelog + provenance summary
- optional handoff packets:
  - marketing brief
  - editor brief
  - legal/IP disclosure notes (non-advice)

Ship Pack is a product feature because it turns “a pile of notes” into “a deliverable.”
